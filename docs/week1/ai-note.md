# Week 1 AI Note

The Week 1 handout allows AI use for brainstorming and requires the prompt and generated ideas to be logged.

## Brainstorming prompt used

> Brainstorm five small, local business or organization problems that could be solved by a CRUD-based application for a five-person student team. Each idea should have 2–4 related record types and be small enough to complete in 12 weeks. Give a one-sentence description for each idea.

## Candidate ideas produced

1. **School supply store inventory and sales** — manage school supplies, suppliers, customers, stock, and sales.
2. **Community library lending tracker** — manage books, members, loans, and returns.
3. **Barbershop appointment tracker** — manage customers, barbers, services, and appointments.
4. **Small bakery order tracker** — manage products, customers, orders, and order items.
5. **Tutoring-center session scheduler** — manage students, tutors, subjects, and sessions.

## Team selection

The team selected **School Supply Store Inventory and Sales Management System**.

The choice is already reflected in this repository and is suitable for the handout's CRUD requirements because the system manages related product, supplier, customer, and transaction records.

## Note

AI was used only for the Week 1 brainstorming activity documented above. The project selection and final scope are the team's decision.
